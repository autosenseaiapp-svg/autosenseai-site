AutoSenseAI

Your AI-powered vehicle assistant. AutoSenseAI helps vehicle owners understand problems with their vehicle and keep track of everything related to owning it, in one app for Android.

What the app does

  • AI vehicle diagnosis: describe a problem using text, photos or audio recordings of unusual sounds. The app asks follow-up questions and suggests possible causes, a severity level and a summary you can share with your mechanic. Results are advisory and do not replace a professional inspection.
  • My Garage: save your vehicles and their details.
  • Fuel tracking: log fill-ups and see fuel economy.
  • Maintenance and reminders: record services and get reminders when maintenance is due.
  • Repairs and expenses: track repair history and vehicle costs.
  • Documents: store vehicle documents such as insurance and licence details.
  • Quote checker: review mechanic quotations with AI assistance.
  • Monthly summaries: get an AI-generated overview of your vehicle costs and activity.

How we use your data

Google Sign-In: when you choose "Sign in with Google", AutoSenseAI receives only your name, email address and profile picture. We use these solely to create your account, sign you in securely and show your profile in the app. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

Vehicle data: the vehicle details, records, photos and audio you add are stored securely and are only accessible to your account. Diagnosis inputs are sent to our secure server for AI analysis to generate your results.

We do not sell your personal data. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
