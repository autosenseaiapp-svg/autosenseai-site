Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Who we are AutoSense AI ("we", "us", "our") is developed and operated by ideagap. AutoSense AI is an AI-powered vehicle assistant app that helps you diagnose vehicle problems and track fuel, maintenance, repairs, expenses, reminders, and vehicle documents. This policy explains what personal information the AutoSense AI mobile app and its admin panel collect, why, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. 2. Information we collect Account information. Email address and password (if you sign up with email), or your Google account identifier, name, email, and profile photo (if you use Sign in with Google). Display name and profile photo you add in Settings. Consent records for AI processing of your data. Vehicle information ("My Garage"). Make, model, year, engine details, fuel type, transmission, current mileage, and a vehicle photo, for each vehicle you add. AI diagnosis data. When you use the Diagnose My Car feature we collect whatever you choose to submit: text descriptions of symptoms you type; photos you capture or upload (e.g. warning lights, tyres, leaks, engine bay, damage); audio recordings of vehicle sounds (e.g. knocking, squealing, grinding) that you record in-app; contextual answers you provide (e.g. cold/warm engine, idling, accelerating, turning) and answers to AI follow-up questions; the resulting AI-generated diagnosis (likely causes, severity, recommended checks, mechanic-ready summary); and repair outcomes you later record against a diagnosis (what a mechanic found, parts replaced, cost, whether it was resolved). Fuel, maintenance, expense, and document records. Fuel entries: quantity, fuel type, price, total cost, odometer reading, station, and receipt photos. Maintenance entries: service date/mileage, category, parts, labour/total cost, workshop, receipt photos, and next-service reminders. Expense entries associated with your vehicles. Scanned or uploaded documents such as receipts, service/repair invoices, mechanic quotations, insurance, and registration documents (including any personal or vehicle information visible in those images). Voice recordings and photos you submit to AI Quick Add or the document/receipt scanner, used only to extract structured data, which is always shown to you for confirmation before anything is saved. Subscription and purchase information. Your subscription plan/tier (Free or Pro) and entitlement status. Purchase and renewal events are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play and managed via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details — payment is handled entirely by Apple/Google. Notifications. A device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver maintenance and document-expiry reminders. Reminder preferences and in-app notification history. Usage and technical data. App usage events needed to enforce fair-use AI limits (e.g. number of diagnoses, scans, or summaries used in a period) and to troubleshoot errors. Standard technical data such as device type, OS version, and crash/error logs. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers that sell your data. Admin panel. Our administrators use a separate, access-controlled admin panel to monitor app health, AI usage, and subscriptions, and to provide support. Admin access is restricted to authorized personnel and enforced at the database level, independent of anything the mobile app sends. 3. How AI diagnosis processes your data Text, photos, audio, and document images you submit for AI features are sent from the app to our secure backend, which forwards the request to the AI API to generate a diagnosis, extract document data, or produce a summary. Our AI API key is never present in the mobile app, all AI calls happen server-side. AI-generated results (possible causes, severity, recommended checks, mechanic-ready summaries, extracted receipt/quote fields) are advisory only and are not a substitute for a professional mechanical inspection. For safety-critical symptoms (brakes, steering, fuel leaks, overheating, major engine or suspension issues) the app will recommend professional inspection. AI-extracted data from Quick Add and document scanning is always shown to you for review and requires your explicit confirmation before it is saved — nothing is saved silently. 4. How we use your information We use your information to: create and manage your account and vehicles; provide AI vehicle diagnosis, follow-up questions, quote checking, document scanning, AI Quick Add, and AI monthly summaries; calculate fuel economy, spending totals, and maintenance reminders; send maintenance and document-expiry push notifications you've enabled; manage your subscription and enforce Free/Pro usage limits; secure your account, prevent abuse, and enforce per-user AI usage limits; provide customer support and respond to your requests; and improve the reliability and safety of the app (e.g. reviewing anonymized error logs) — never by selling your personal data. 5. Who we share information with We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers needed to run AutoSense AI, each acting under their own privacy and security terms: - Database — authentication, database, and file storage for all app data (account data, vehicle data, diagnosis/fuel/maintenance/expense/document records, uploaded photos/audio). - AI API — AI diagnosis, document/quote understanding, AI Quick Add extraction, monthly summaries (text you submit, photos, audio, extracted document contents — sent via our server, never with your Google account directly). - Google Sign-In — optional account sign-in method (name, email, profile photo, Google account identifier). - RevenueCat — managing subscription entitlements across iOS and Android (subscription/purchase status, app user identifier). - Apple App Store / Google Play — processing in-app subscription payments; payment is handled entirely by Apple/Google, we never see your card details. We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of AutoSense AI, our users, or the public. 6. Data storage and security All app data is stored in secure database and file storage, protected by Row Level Security policies so that each user can only access their own records. Uploaded photos, audio recordings, and documents are stored in access-controlled storage buckets and are not publicly accessible. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our AI and admin-privileged operations run only in server-side functions — sensitive keys are never shipped inside the mobile app. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security. 7. Data retention and account deletion We retain your account and vehicle data for as long as your account is active, so the app can maintain your vehicle history, reminders, and diagnosis history. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Delete account in the app; this removes your profile and triggers deletion of your associated data and uploaded files (photos, audio, documents) from our storage. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes (e.g. subscription/purchase records), after which it is deleted or anonymized. 8. Your rights and choices Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or export the personal data we hold about you; delete your account and associated data; withdraw consent for AI processing (this will limit or disable AI features); object to or restrict certain processing of your information; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. 9. Children's privacy AutoSense AI is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it. 10. Device permissions AutoSense AI requests the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose: Camera — to photograph a symptom, vehicle, receipt, or document for AI diagnosis or scanning. Photo library — to attach existing vehicle, receipt, or document photos. Microphone — to record a vehicle sound (e.g. an engine noise) or a voice note for AI Quick Add. Notifications — to deliver maintenance and document-expiry reminders. You can decline or revoke any of these permissions in your device settings; doing so will limit the related feature but the rest of the app will continue to work. 11. Changes to this policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice in the app. 12.
